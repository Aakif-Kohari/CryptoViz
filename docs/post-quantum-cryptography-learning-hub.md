## Learning coverage

### Kyber / ML-KEM

A lattice-based key encapsulation mechanism for establishing shared secrets.

### Dilithium / ML-DSA

A lattice-based digital signature scheme suitable for general-purpose post-quantum signatures.

### Falcon

A lattice-based signature scheme known for compact signatures and more complex implementation requirements.

### SPHINCS+ / SLH-DSA

A stateless hash-based signature scheme with conservative assumptions and larger signature sizes.
