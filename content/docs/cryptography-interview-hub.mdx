# Cryptography Interview Hub

This hub helps learners prepare for technical interviews by combining multiple-choice questions, flashcards, and practical security scenarios.

---

## Topics Covered

- Symmetric Encryption
- Asymmetric Encryption
- Hash Functions
- Digital Signatures
- TLS
- PKI
- Key Exchange
- Secure Protocols

---

## Preparation Tips

- Understand the difference between encryption and hashing.
- Learn where AES, RSA, ECC, SHA, and TLS are used.
- Practice explaining concepts rather than memorizing definitions.
